## How to open the models

- **Quickest look:** drag any `viewer.glb` onto
  https://gltf-viewer.donmccurdy.com — nothing to install.
- **To edit:** open `scene.blend` in Blender 4.5 or later (free, blender.org).
  The outliner has `MASTER_FLOWER`, `PIECE_A` and `PIECE_B` as separate
  collections; each piece is an independently selectable object.

## Please read

- **These concepts are provisional.** AFRi's reference material was not
  available when they were made. Colours, proportions and construction are
  studio assumptions, not brand specifications.
- **The two pieces are genuinely separate solids.** They are not one model with
  a line drawn on it. Each exports, opens and moves independently, and together
  they reconstruct the complete flower.
- **No hat is included.** Hat modelling, placement and attachment are the next
  stage and have not been started.
- **These are not manufacturing files.** Wall thickness, tolerances and process
  constraints have not been assessed. Please treat the STL and OBJ files as
  design geometry to review, not as production data.
