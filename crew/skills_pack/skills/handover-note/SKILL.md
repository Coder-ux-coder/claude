# Handover notes

Assume the next reader has none of your context and cannot ask you. Write, in this order:

- **Done:** what now works (and how you know).
- **Decided:** choices you made and why (names, formats, edge-case handling).
- **Next:** the very next step, concretely (file and function).
- **Watch out:** anything surprising — a flaky test, a quirk of a library, a trap you fell into.

Two to six lines. Update at each milestone, not every step.
