A LinkedIn URL column is all that is required. Adding Name, Clinic or Domain columns raises the match rate substantially — a bare profile URL often will not resolve on its own.

How it works

  1. Identity — resolve the profile to a name, role and clinic.
  2. Email — try each provider in order; the first hit wins.
  3. Validation — a catch-all or unknown result is never counted as confirmed.
  4. Phone — published business numbers only, each with the URL that proves it.
  5. Review — anything doubtful is queued for you rather than shipped.

You get three files: the six delivery columns, a full audit trail, and the review queue.
